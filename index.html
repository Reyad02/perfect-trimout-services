04 / Frequently asked

Good questions deserve
clear answers

Here is what clients commonly want to know before sending a manufactured or modular home project request.

What type of homes does Perfect Trim Out Services support?+

We support manufactured and modular homes after delivery, at a customer property, retail center, display location or community. Singlewide and doublewide trim-out packages are available for VOG wall panels, partial drywall, and full drywall scopes.

What is included in a trim-out package?+

Approved trim-out work may include marriage-line repairs, trim, touch-up painting, cabinet, and door adjustments, ship-loose items, assigned punch items, interior deep cleaning, final quality review, project photos, and a completion walk-through. The exact work is confirmed in the approved scope.

Can you handle several repair issues in one service call?+

Yes. Send the issue list, room or location, known quantities, photos, and the target outcome. Service calls can cover approved flooring, drywall, and texture, paint, doors, jambs, baseboards, windows, hardware, tub or surround repairs, and roof patching.

Do you provide decks, skirting, and rehab work?+

Yes, these are handled through project-specific written proposals. Helpful details include home dimensions, deck length, and width, height, skirting perimeter, material preferences, steps or landings, and clear site photos.

How is project completion documented?+

Trim-outs use the assigned walk-through checklist, organized before/during/after photos, and a completion punch list. Service calls use a Contractor Service Report. Photos and project records are organized through the approved project documentation workflow.

Is my requested completion date guaranteed?+

No. A requested date helps the team review scheduling, but it is not a confirmed dispatch. Timing is confirmed only after the scope, site readiness, access, materials, and crew availability have been reviewed.
